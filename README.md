# About Me

I am an aspiring Frontend Developer from Ukraine, currently living in Italy.

I am approaching development consciously: I practice a lot, study modern approaches, and gradually build my own projects. I don’t have commercial experience yet, but I have discipline, a strong interest in technology, and a desire to grow in this field.

As a child, when I was not allowed to use the computer, I once installed a program on CD-RW that bypassed the Windows password. At the time, it seemed like a prank, but later I realized it was my first step toward engineering thinking 💻

## 🎓 Education:
- **Master’s degree in International Finance**  
- **Bachelor’s degree in International Tourism**  

But my true passion has always been **computers**.
